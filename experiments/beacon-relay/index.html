Beacon Relay

Guide a service rover across three original courses. Gather a light and reach each beacon.

Arrows move / aim · Z or Space jump · X dash · P pause. Jump or dash starts a new relay. A dash recharges on landing. Click the game to focus it.

Checkpoints remember the next course after you continue from a beacon. They do not save your position, light count, retries or elapsed time. This edition is intentionally silent.
