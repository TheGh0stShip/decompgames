Kiln

Find the yellow key, bring the power stone to R, then touch F to finish. An original four-board puzzle running in the native Super ZZT source port. Sound off.

Click the game to focus. Enter closes the introduction, P plays, arrows move, S saves and R restores from the start menu. After Play or Restore, move to resume. Escape, then Y returns to the menu; P restarts.

Wait for “Checkpoint saved locally” before closing. Saves stay in this browser; clearing site data removes them.
