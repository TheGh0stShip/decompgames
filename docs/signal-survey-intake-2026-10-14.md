# Signal Survey intake and authored campaign

Signal Survey adds a ROM-free **Rogue developer-source port** with an independently authored three-station campaign. The catalog is **90**; the strict additional-decompilation milestone remains **4/5**. This entry is not a matching decompilation, emulation, the original Rogue dungeon or a clean-room certificate.

The pinned reference is `Davidslv/rogue` at `f4653c2a2ee6981a73abe9dfda055134285e1e79`. Its original-game BSD-style grant names Michael Toy, Ken Arnold and Glenn Wichman; separate save/crypt notices are retained. The 48-file raw text snapshot remains hash-identical. No upstream PNG, archive, executable, ROM or audio was fetched. The maintainer's mirror provenance does not constitute an independently verified original release-history certificate.

The authored campaign replaces procedural level generation with three fixed layouts, six initial native actor encounters, repair vials, ration packs and a native lens pickup/return objective. Rogue movement, collision, actor AI, health, combat, inventory, item effects and ascent victory remain native C. Wandering spawns are disabled for this bounded course. The course builder guards its three-entry table against out-of-range native state. Earlier native daemon-array and room/passage save fixes remain in the derived BSD save implementation.
